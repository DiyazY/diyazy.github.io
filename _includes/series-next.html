{%- comment -%} The "Next in the series" title in an After the Feed part's closing line. Links to that part once it is published (in site.posts), plain bold text before, so a forward link never 404s and nobody edits the previous part on reveal day: the Wednesday build that publishes Part N also turns Part N-1's title into a link. Parts are numbered by date, as on /after-the-feed.html. Params: part (1-based part number), title (the bold text). Output is a single line with no surrounding whitespace, because it sits inside the post's Markdown italic line. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign series = site.posts | where_exp: "p", "p.tags contains 'after-the-feed'" | sort: "date" -%} {%- assign index = include.part | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign next_part = series[index] -%} {%- if next_part -%}{{ include.title }}{%- else -%}{{ include.title }}{%- endif -%}
