After the Feed: Rebuilding Social Media

Somewhere in the last decade, social apps quietly changed jobs — from connecting you to people you know into keeping you watching whatever a machine ranked highest. This series is a field guide to what broke and what’s being built in its place: the pattern behind the last three social takeovers, the small apps already proving people want out, the openings nobody has fully built yet, and — for anyone who wants the mechanics — how you’d actually build a network that runs on trust and human presence instead of attention, and how it could pay for itself without selling you. Read it in order, or jump in anywhere.
