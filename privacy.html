Privacy

This is a personal site run by {{ site.author.name }}. It collects as little as it can. Last updated September 24, 2026.

If you subscribe by email

What I store: your email address and which topics you chose ("New posts", and "Programmes" only if you ticked the box). Your address is added to the list only after you click the link in the confirmation email. If you never click it, no subscriber record is created, though the confirmation email itself stays in Resend's delivery log for a while.

Why: to send you what you asked for. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the link at the bottom of every email.

Who processes it:

  • Resend (Resend, Inc.) keeps the subscriber list and delivers the emails, sending from its EU region.
  • Cloudflare runs the small service behind the form and the Turnstile check that tells people from bots. Turnstile looks at signals from your browser for that one purpose; in this setup it sets no cookies.

How long: while you're subscribed. After you unsubscribe, your address is kept only as a do-not-email record, so nothing is ever sent to it again; ask me and I'll delete it completely. Emails have no open or click tracking.

Analytics

The site uses PostHog (EU cloud) in cookieless mode: no cookies, no local storage, IP addresses anonymised, and a visitor identifier that is a daily-rotating hash. It records things like page views, searches and whether a post was read to the end, so I can see what's useful.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what's stored about you, or object to its use. If you're a subscriber, reply to any email; otherwise message me on LinkedIn. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Finland, the Data Protection Ombudsman).
