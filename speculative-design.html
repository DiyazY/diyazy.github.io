--- layout: series nav_parent: /series.html title: "Speculative Design: Harnessing Human Bioenergy" description: "A speculative design series on turning the human body — flatulence, sweat, motion — into energy: the why, the hypothetical mechanics, the social and ethical stakes, and where human-powered technology could lead." image: /assets/images/posts/2025-03-10-Speculative-Design-Fart-Reactor/img-01.png card_image: /assets/images/tiles/speculative-design.webp series_tag: speculative-design title_prefix: "Speculative Design | " numbered_from: 0 --- It started as a joke — a wearable that runs on flatulence — and turned into a seven-part thought experiment about the human body as a power source. What begins with a “fart reactor” ends somewhere serious: the mechanics of capturing bodily energy, the taboos and social norms in the way, the other outputs (sweat, motion, heat) already being harvested in labs, and the ethics of who owns the data your body makes. Speculative design doesn’t predict the future; it builds an absurd object so the ordinary one sneaking up behind it becomes visible. Read it in order, or jump in anywhere.
