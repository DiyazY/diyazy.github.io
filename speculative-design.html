Speculative Design: Harnessing Human Bioenergy

It started as a joke — a wearable that runs on flatulence — and turned into a seven-part thought experiment about the human body as a power source. What begins with a “fart reactor” ends somewhere serious: the mechanics of capturing bodily energy, the taboos and social norms in the way, the other outputs (sweat, motion, heat) already being harvested in labs, and the ethics of who owns the data your body makes. Speculative design doesn’t predict the future; it builds an absurd object so the ordinary one sneaking up behind it becomes visible. Read it in order, or jump in anywhere.
